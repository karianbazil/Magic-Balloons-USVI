UI / UX Designer & Devloper

Hi my name is Karian Bazil, I am a Computer Science student in my last semester at Georgia State University. My website Magic Balloons USVI, was built for a small business my mom owns in the U.S. Virgin Islands. She opened it a few years ago, when I was in high school, to help cover my college expenses. The business centers around custom balloon arrangements for special events. This includes but is not limited to balloon arches balloon bouquets, balloon bundles, and whatever else the customer's heart desires.

My goal in building this website is to give back to my mother and help her business grow with an online presence. It is also a great opportunity to improve upon my front end development skills by building something with a real purpose behind it. As a CS student with limited professional experience but big aspirations, creating something meaningful to me is a way to stand out on my journey as a developer.
